# A Score for Two Hands — Human × AI

**In less than one year: from discovering AI to building practical tools and creative projects.**

This project documents a real learning journey. AI became more than a conversational assistant: a way to investigate, learn technical skills, debug problems, develop small tools, organize working knowledge, and explore a science-fiction world through writing and comics. The human author retains intent, decisions, fact-checking, and final approval.

> **“AI does not replace my ideas or decisions. It helps me turn them into things I can build.”**

## The method

**Construct → verify → correct → explicitly approve → document the lesson (RETEX).**

RETEX handovers record validated results, unresolved issues, rejected alternatives, source references and the next action. Across separate conversations, continuity is achieved by reading persistent project records and reconciling their status. This is **documented continuity**, not a claim of perfect automatic memory.

## What is public — and what is not

The gallery contains **only the cover and boards 01–11** (including 05A and 05B): 13 physical pages from a previously approved edition of *La Griffe Noire*. The current comic is being rebuilt. No complete manuscript, full canon, raw conversations, infrastructure configuration, personal network addresses, credentials or private repository files are disclosed. The Linux guide is a curated public edition, not a raw export of the private master document.
